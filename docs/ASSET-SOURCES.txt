1. User-supplied study

Source files:
Reaction_Time_30_Participants_Final.pdf
Reaction_Time_30_Participants_Final.docx

Study title: Background Conversation and Visual Reaction Time
30 participants; 298 retained trials (150 Quiet, 148 Conversation).

Report provenance:
Page 1: research question, experimental method and cleaning.
Page 2: Figure 1 histograms and Figure 2 cumulative relative-frequency curves.
Page 3: Figure 3 box plots and Table 1 pooled statistics.
Page 4: Table 2 participant means and reported paired differences.
Page 5: paired summary, conclusion and limitations.

assets/histogram.png and assets/cumulative.png preserve the report's original Figure 1 and Figure 2. No trial-level frequency distribution was invented or reconstructed. assets/boxplot-original.png is a source reference; the displayed box plot is redrawn from Table 1.

charts.js redraws box plots, paired difference charts and appendix tables from reported values. Participant differences are retained as reported; small discrepancies from subtracting the displayed means may result from independent rounding. No complete trial-level dataset was provided, and no significance test is presented.

2. Additional analysis

The sensitivity comparison is approximate and uses the rounded Conversation mean:
(148 × 253.0 + 2030.5 + 1973.4) ÷ 150 = approximately 276.3 ms.

The comparison Quiet mean is 261.8 ms. This demonstrates sensitivity of the pooled mean comparison to the two stated exclusions; it does not establish that every exclusion was incorrect or reconstruct a raw-trial distribution.

3. Group attribution and fictional narrative

Project attribution checked against Teams:
A1 Maths Investigation, Group 1.
Ethan Yang; Aries Xia; Hector Kang; Rayman Guo.

The report does not establish participant recruitment or a randomised/balanced condition order. The deck does not claim those procedures. The 3D room and apparatus are a conceptual illustration, not photographs or an exact reconstruction of the study setup. Nathan represents a fictional investigator, not a participant, group member or quoted real speaker.

The opening shows five preset animation examples: 248, 231, 267, 219 and 243 ms. They are explicitly labelled as example timings, not study data. They are not participant measurements or live audience testing. Sleep, practice, time of day, device and background sound are candidate ideas, not established study findings. Research on the computer means reviewing the supplied report and analysis, not presenting external literature findings. The conclusion dialogue is presentation narration. The closing shows an animated press of the model keyboard's Enter key to confirm an on-screen shutdown dialog. The final thank-you page then moves the chair back, stands the character up and uses an approximately 30-degree bow, pause and return to upright; this 12-second action adds no study data.

There are 15 main slides and two reference appendices. Main-slide timers total 540 seconds (9 minutes); transitions add approximately half a minute. Dialogue is silent text for the presenter to read aloud.
